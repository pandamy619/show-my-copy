One-time codes from Google Authenticator: Settings → Codes keeps the accounts of your two-factor logins, and the history window shows their current codes with a countdown in a Codes section under the search bar, hidden until you ask (⌘K). Enter copies a code; it is never recorded in the history and leaves the clipboard after 30 seconds. Add accounts from an otpauth:// address, a bare key or a screenshot of the Google Authenticator export, read on this Mac. Secrets are encrypted with the same key as saved passwords and never leave this Mac.

Security fixes from an audit: the Settings window is kept out of screen sharing and recordings, history saved by a newer version is left untouched, a history that will not open is set aside only when SQLite finds it damaged, styled text previews cannot ask for a slow layout, and paste checks that the same process is still in front.
